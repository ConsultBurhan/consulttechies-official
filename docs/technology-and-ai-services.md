## Overview

We help businesses design, build, connect, operate, and improve their digital services. Our portfolio spans WhatsApp-based customer journeys, websites and mobile applications, custom software, cloud and DevOps, data quality, platform integration, customer engagement, and technology advisory.

Artificial intelligence can strengthen these services where it fits the business need: supporting natural-language interactions, assisting customer service teams, helping users find information, summarizing data, identifying patterns, supporting workflow automation, and making business systems easier to use. AI capabilities are scoped to the client’s data, systems, security requirements, and operational processes.

### Our approach

- Understand the business objective, users, current systems, and constraints.

- Design a practical solution that fits the existing environment where possible.

- Build and integrate the required applications, automations, data flows, and AI capabilities.

- Test functionality, access controls, reliability, and user experience before release.

- Support ongoing operation, maintenance, and continuous improvement where agreed.

## AI across the service portfolio

AI is not a standalone add-on to every project. It is a capability that can be applied selectively to improve the experience or efficiency of a defined workflow. Depending on the use case, this may include conversational assistants, classification and extraction, content assistance, knowledge search, data analysis, recommendation support, or AI-assisted monitoring and troubleshooting.

### Examples of AI-enabled outcomes

- Let customers ask questions in natural language and receive responses grounded in approved business information.

- Assist support teams by summarizing conversations, finding relevant knowledge, and suggesting responses for review.

- Use AI to classify requests, extract information from supported documents, and route tasks to the appropriate workflow.

- Turn business questions into structured analysis, summaries, charts, or draft reports when connected data and permissions allow.

- Help teams identify recurring issues and operational patterns from available, appropriately governed data.

AI outputs should be validated for the intended use. Integrations, automation, data access, and any human approval steps are defined during solution design.

## How we scope a solution

Each engagement is tailored to the business objective, current technology, available data, user needs, and operating constraints. A typical engagement may include the following stages:

- Discovery: define the problem, stakeholders, requirements, and success criteria.

- Solution design: agree on architecture, integrations, security, data handling, and user experience.

- Build and configuration: implement the agreed application, platform, automation, or AI components.

- Validation: test key workflows, permissions, edge cases, reliability, and expected outputs.

- Launch and handover: prepare documentation, user guidance, and operational ownership.

- Ongoing improvement: review feedback and agreed measures to identify next improvements.

## Security, governance, and responsible AI

Security and governance requirements should be defined for each solution. Depending on the project, this can include role-based access, least-privilege permissions, approved data sources, logging, retention rules, secure integration credentials, and human approval for sensitive actions. AI features should have a defined purpose, evaluation approach, fallback behavior, and clear boundaries on what data and actions they can access.

Specific controls, certifications, service levels, platform approvals, and compliance commitments are confirmed during discovery and documented in the relevant proposal or service agreement. They should not be assumed unless explicitly agreed.
