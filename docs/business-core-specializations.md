## How the four specializations work together

The strongest solutions often connect more than one of these areas. For example, a WhatsApp customer journey can connect to a CRM, use AI to interpret customer enquiries, and rely on a maintained application and integration layer behind the scenes. We can deliver a focused service or combine capabilities into a broader solution based on the business objective.

- WhatsApp + CRM: connect customer conversations, lead records, campaign activity, and support workflows.

- WhatsApp + AI: support natural-language enquiries and guided customer journeys with appropriate fallback and handover.

- AI + business platforms: bring approved knowledge, data analysis, and workflow assistance into existing systems.

- CRM + data quality: improve customer records and segmentation before activating journeys or campaigns.

- DevOps + application management: support reliable deployment, monitoring, maintenance, and continuous improvement.

## How we deliver

- Discover: clarify business goals, user needs, current systems, and constraints.

- Design: define the solution, integrations, data flows, security boundaries, and success measures.

- Implement: configure platforms, develop software, build automation, or integrate AI as agreed.

- Validate: test functionality, access permissions, edge cases, and operational readiness.

- Launch and support: document the solution, hand over operational guidance, and provide support according to scope.

- Improve: use feedback and agreed measures to prioritize the next improvements.
